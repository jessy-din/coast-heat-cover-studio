An offline web app that turns your photos and a caption into GTA-style multi-panel cover art — a sunset poster with bold 3D lettering, a neon script subtitle, star meter, badge and ticker strip — exported as a PNG to share. Made for the make.fun competition gta6-cover-art-01 ("Make GTA 6 Cover Art"). Everything in it is original: the sample photos were made with an image model for this studio, the lettering is Anton and Pacifico under the SIL Open Font License, and no game's logo, artwork or fonts are used anywhere.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

1. Pick a sample photo or add your own (photos never leave your machine).
2. Choose one of 6 layouts and 6 sunset grades.
3. Export the cover as a PNG in 3 sizes.
